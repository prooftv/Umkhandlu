# Umkhandlu — Commercial Rate Card
## Unami Foundation | Platform Operator

---

## Pricing Principle

The traditional council pays nothing. The platform is free for the council.

External parties — developers, contractors, municipalities, attorneys, businesses — pay Unami Foundation for services delivered through the platform.

---

## Tier 1: Public Participation & Notice Hosting

**Target:** Environmental Assessment Practitioners (EAPs), developers, attorneys

**What they get:**
- 30–60 day hosting of EIA / Basic Assessment Report / SPLUMA notice
- Localised digital notice board pinned to the traditional authority area
- Public comments submission form (structured, exportable)
- Proof of Publication certificate (printable PDF for court/authority submission)

**Pricing:** R2,500 – R4,500 per notice

**Why:** Traditional print media display ads cost R6,000–R12,000 for a single day. Umkhandlu delivers multi-month community reach at a fraction of the cost, with better local precision and a structured evidence trail.

---

## Tier 2: Active Monitoring & Community Grievance Redress

**Target:** Municipalities (district or local), industrial contractors, water services

**What they get:**
- Dedicated project page with live progress tracking
- Community complaint/grievance capture (structured, timestamped)
- EPWP employment transparency (phase-by-phase hiring visibility)
- Traditional council notification channel (Induna oversight layer)
- Monthly progress log updates
- Evidence preservation (TCRS — all source reports retained)

**Pricing:** R1,500 – R3,000 per month (SaaS subscription)

**Why:** A dedicated CLO / labour desk service provider bills R12,000–R25,000/month to manually manage job applications and community disputes. Umkhandlu digitises that function at a fraction of the cost, with a permanent audit trail.

---

## Tier 3: Full Infrastructure Documentation Package

**Target:** Major contractors, mining operations, national government departments

**What they get:**
- Full project lifecycle documentation (procurement → construction → commissioning → operational)
- Digital Project Information Board (DPWI/CIDB format)
- Milestone-based progress tracking (engineer-certified completions)
- EPWP socio-economic targets (employment + SMME tracking)
- End-of-project compliance export (PDF audit trail)
- Traditional council acknowledgment record
- Legally defensible evidence log (publication dates, community comments, verification records)

**Pricing:** R12,000 – R18,000 once-off (full project duration)

**Why:** Enterprises allocate significant budgets to ESG legal compliance. A foolproof, auditable data log protects against litigation and project shutdowns by the Department of Water and Sanitation or NEMA enforcement.
