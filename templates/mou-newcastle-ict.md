## 2. PURPOSE

This Memorandum of Understanding establishes a collaborative framework between the Newcastle Municipality ICT Department and the Unami Foundation for:

1. **Community Engagement Platform (Umkhandlu)** — External community-facing transparency and participation layer for municipal infrastructure projects within traditional authority areas.

2. **IT Project Management System (ITPMS)** — Collaborative development of the municipality's internal ICT project governance tool.

3. **System Integration** — Connecting internal municipal project management processes with external community visibility outputs.

---

## 3. BACKGROUND

The Newcastle Municipality ICT Department requires:
- An internal project management system for ICT governance (ITPMS)
- A community engagement mechanism for infrastructure projects in traditional authority areas

The Unami Foundation has developed and deployed:
- **Umkhandlu** — A community-scale institutional memory platform currently piloted on the Buffalo River Abstraction Works (Ward 7), operating under the mandate of the Khathide Traditional Council

Both parties recognise that these systems are complementary:
- ITPMS handles internal governance (budget, procurement, resources, audit)
- Umkhandlu handles external transparency (community notices, participation, evidence preservation, compliance)

---

## 4. SCOPE OF COLLABORATION

### 4.1 Umkhandlu (Community Engagement Platform)

**Unami Foundation shall:**
- Operate and maintain the Umkhandlu platform for community engagement on municipal infrastructure projects
- Provide community notice publication services (public participation, statutory notices)
- Generate Proof of Publication certificates for compliance
- Document project progress using milestone-based verification
- Facilitate public participation and capture community feedback
- Maintain the evidence preservation layer (TCRS)
- Coordinate with traditional council authorities (Khathide Traditional Council)

**The Municipality shall:**
- Provide project information for public-facing documentation (milestones, employment data, media statements)
- Recognise Umkhandlu as an official community engagement channel for projects in traditional authority areas
- Facilitate the MOU approval through municipal governance channels
- Provide feedback on platform requirements for municipal reporting alignment

### 4.2 ITPMS (Internal Project Management System)

**Unami Foundation shall:**
- Provide frontend development services for the ITPMS platform
- Deliver UI/UX implementation aligned with the Functional Requirements Specification (FRS v1.0)
- Collaborate with the ICT Department on system architecture and integration

**The Municipality shall:**
- Provide the backend development and database architecture
- Provide the Functional Requirements Specification and ongoing requirements clarification
- Provide infrastructure for internal deployment (servers, Active Directory, etc.)
- Manage internal user onboarding and role-based access configuration

### 4.3 Integration Layer

Both parties shall collaborate on connecting the two systems where appropriate:

| Internal (ITPMS) | External (Umkhandlu) | Integration |
|---|---|---|
| Project milestone certified | → Published on community page | API or manual operator update |
| Procurement awarded | → SMME directory updated | Operator documents |
| Employment targets set | → Info Board displays targets | Operator publishes |
| Project status updated | → Community notice issued | Operator or webhook |
| Budget allocated | → Funding source shown on board | Public info only (no amounts) |

## 7. FINANCIAL ARRANGEMENTS

### 7.1 Umkhandlu

- The municipality pays no licence fee for Umkhandlu visibility on their projects
- Revenue from external parties (developers, attorneys, businesses) using the platform for statutory notices flows to Unami Foundation
- Infrastructure documentation services (Digital Project Info Board, compliance reporting) may be invoiced separately per project at agreed rates

### 7.2 ITPMS

- Frontend development services to be quoted separately based on the FRS scope
- Payment terms: milestone-based (per module delivered and accepted)
- Rates to be agreed in a separate Service Level Agreement (SLA)

---

## 8. PILOT PROJECT

**Buffalo River Abstraction Works (Ward 7)**

Both parties agree to use the Buffalo River project as the pilot integration point:
- Umkhandlu is already live and documenting the project
- ITPMS (when developed) will track the project internally
- The integration between the two systems will be tested on this project first

**Pilot Period:** 6 months from date of signature

---

## 9. DATA PROTECTION (POPIA)

- Umkhandlu does not store personal data of community members in the CMS
- Public participation comments are delivered via webhook to the operator only
- The participation log stores anonymised summaries (type, date, action taken)
- ITPMS shall comply with POPIA as specified in the FRS (Section 20)
- Neither system shall share personal data between internal and external layers without consent

---

## 10. DURATION AND REVIEW

- This MOU is effective from the date of signature
- Initial period: 12 months
- Reviewed quarterly by both parties
- Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice
- All community data on Umkhandlu remains accessible to the traditional council regardless of MOU status

---

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Any disputes arising from this MOU shall be resolved through:
1. Direct consultation between designated representatives
2. Mediation (if consultation fails)
3. The terms of this MOU do not create a binding legal contract — it establishes a framework for collaboration
