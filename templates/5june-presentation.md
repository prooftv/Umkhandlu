# Umkhandlu — Presentation for Khathide Traditional Council
## 5 June 2026 | Osizweni / Flathela

---

## What This Is

**Umkhandlu** is a digital platform built specifically for traditional councils.

It is not a website. It is not a government system. It is not owned by the municipality.

> It is the digital council office of the Khathide Traditional Council — owned and controlled by the council, operated by Unami Foundation on the council's behalf.

---

## Why This Protects the Council

### 1. Employment Transparency

The project promises 100 local jobs. The community will ask:

> "Where are the jobs? Who is getting them? Why not us?"

With Umkhandlu, the council can publish:
- How many positions are open
- Which phase of hiring is active
- How to register interest
- When the next phase opens

This prevents the information gap that causes community conflict and work stoppages.

### 2. The Council's Voice Is Permanent

Once the council adopts this platform, every project in the area is linked to the council's authority. The municipality cannot claim they didn't consult the traditional leadership — because the entire project record is stamped with the council's oversight.

### 3. Protection Against False Claims

If a contractor claims 70% completion but the community sees nothing happening — the platform records both claims. The council has evidence. The community has evidence. The contractor cannot simply change the story.

## What Is Being Asked of the Council Today

One resolution. One letter.

**Resolution:** The Khathide Traditional Council officially adopts Umkhandlu as its digital council office and primary instrument for community engagement and project monitoring.

**Letter:** The Induna issues a letter to Newcastle Municipality PMU, the consulting engineer, and the contractor requesting that project updates, EPWP employment numbers, and milestones be shared with the platform for community transparency.

These two documents give the council institutional authority over the digital record of everything happening in this area.

## The Bigger Picture

This is not just about Buffalo River.

Every development that comes into this area — every EIA, every rezoning, every mining application, every cell tower — will require public notification. The platform becomes the official digital notice channel for the Khathide Traditional Council area.

Developers and attorneys currently pay R6,000–R12,000 for a single newspaper notice that most community members never see. Through this platform, they pay a fraction of that — and the notice reaches every resident with a phone, in their area, in their language.

The council's authority grows with every notice published.
