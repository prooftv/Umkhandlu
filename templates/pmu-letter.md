## RE: Buffalo River Abstraction Works — Request for Information Cooperation

Dear Sir/Madam,

The Khathide Traditional Council has adopted a digital community platform (**Umkhandlu**) as its official instrument for community engagement, project monitoring, and public record-keeping.

This platform serves the communities of Mndozo, Manzana, Dicks, Jobstown, and Johnstone — the direct beneficiaries of the Buffalo River Abstraction Works project.

### Purpose

The Council requires project visibility for the following reasons:

1. **Employment transparency** — To prevent disputes regarding EPWP labour allocation and rotation
2. **Progress accountability** — To keep the community informed of construction milestones
3. **Public participation** — To fulfil the community's right to information regarding developments affecting their area
4. **Institutional memory** — To maintain a permanent record of project commitments and outcomes

### Request

The Council respectfully requests that the following information be made available to the platform operator (Unami Foundation) on a regular basis:

| Information | Frequency | Source |
|---|---|---|
| EPWP employment numbers (local labour count) | Monthly | CLO / Contractor |
| Project phase updates | When phase changes | Engineer / PMU |
| Construction milestones achieved | As certified | Engineer |
| Local SMME appointments | When confirmed | Contractor |
| Site photographs (progress evidence) | Monthly | CLO / Contractor |
| Any public notices or community communications | As issued | PMU |

### Assurance

The platform:

- Does not publish confidential commercial information
- Does not publish budget or tender pricing details
- Does not interfere with project management or engineering decisions
- Only publishes information that is already public or community-facing
- Operates under Council authority, not independently

### Authority

This request is issued under the authority of the Khathide Traditional Council Resolution dated ___________________, which adopted the Umkhandlu platform as the Council's official digital record.

The Council views this cooperation as essential to maintaining peace, preventing employment disputes, and ensuring that the community benefits promised by this project are delivered transparently.
