## RESOLUTION

The Khathide Traditional Council, under the authority of Induna _________________, hereby resolves:

### 1. Platform Adoption

The Council officially adopts the **Umkhandlu** digital platform as its primary instrument for:

- Community notice publication
- Infrastructure project monitoring and documentation
- Public participation record-keeping
- Governance decision archiving
- Development notice publication
- Community directory and mapping

### 2. Operational Authority

The platform shall be operated by the **Unami Foundation** as Council Operating Partner (COP) under the following terms:

- The Council retains full governance authority over all published content
- The platform operates under Council mandate, not independently
- All data belongs to the Council
- The Council pays no fees for platform access or operation
- Revenue from external parties (developers, attorneys, businesses) flows to Unami Foundation as the platform operator

### 3. Stakeholder Cooperation

The Council requests that all parties involved in infrastructure projects within the Mndozo Traditional Authority area provide project updates, employment data, and milestone information to the platform for the purposes of:

- Community transparency
- Employment dispute prevention
- Progress verification
- Public accountability

This includes but is not limited to:

- Newcastle Municipality Project Management Unit (PMU)
- Appointed consulting engineers
- Main contractors and sub-contractors
- Community Liaison Officers (CLOs)

### 4. Evidence & Record-Keeping

The platform shall serve as the Council's official digital record of:

- Community notices issued
- Infrastructure project progress
- Public participation processes
- Governance decisions and resolutions
- Development applications affecting the area

Records published on the platform constitute the Council's institutional memory and shall not be deleted without Council authority.

### 5. Independence

The platform operator (Unami Foundation) maintains operational independence for the purpose of neutral documentation. The operator:

- Does not make governance decisions
- Does not adjudicate disputes
- Does not represent any contractor, municipality, or political party
- Records, preserves, organises, and presents information only
